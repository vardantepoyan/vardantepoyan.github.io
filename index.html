Vardan Tepoyan

Ph.D. in Mathematics from YSU and KSPEU Current position: Curriculum Methodologist at RSM

Hello! I’m Vardan. I am a mathematician and educator. My mathematical background is in operator algebras and functional analysis. My doctoral research was focused on C*-algebras generated by isometric representations of abelian semigroups.

Much of my professional life has been devoted to mathematics education. I currently work at the Russian School of Mathematics as a Senior Curriculum Methodologist and Senior Math Teacher. I have taught mathematics at many levels, from middle and high school through university courses. My teaching experience includes Precalculus, Calculus, Geometry, Trigonometry, Functional Analysis, Probability Theory, and Statistics.

I have always been interested in understanding difficult problems and finding clear ways to explain them. More recently, I have been expanding my mathematical background into Computer Science and Machine Learning. In particular I am focused on building practical skills in Python and it's corresponding libraries for Machine Learning.
